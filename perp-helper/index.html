Predictions are probabilities built from pattern statistics — barrier distance to your line, volatility, momentum, and each coin's 6-month base rates. They are not guarantees, and short-horizon crypto direction is close to a coin flip: the base models' held-out record is printed on every card, and the scoreboard above shows this page's real record, wins and losses alike. The snipe badge uses the hourly setup only, read on a ~4-hour window: on held-out data across all 20 coins, flagged setups produced the big move 23.1% of the time against a 14.4% unflagged base rate (1.61x lift). The 15-minute snipe tested as noise (lift under 1x) and is not used. Data: Coinbase Exchange public feed. Not financial advice.
